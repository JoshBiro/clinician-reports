Who We Are

Independent Evaluations. Honest Answers.

A team of clinicians and human factors researchers giving you the honest, vendor-free reviews you deserve before choosing an AI scribe tool.

Our Mission

Clinicians deserve honest reviews before buying clinical tools.

We believe clinicians deserve the same thing every consumer takes for granted, the ability to read an honest review before buying a product. In healthcare, that's rarely possible. Clinical tools are complex, workflows are high-stakes, and vendors control most of the information available. We built this resource to change that.

There are almost no independent review platforms focused on the clinical products that front-line providers actually use. These tools are often highly specialized, sold into low-competition markets, and evaluated, if at all, by the companies selling them. We're here to fill that gap with rigorous, vendor-free, provider-focused evaluations.

Our Standards

Editorial Independence

Each evaluation is conducted without vendor involvement, sponsorship, or compensation from any evaluated product. No AI scribe vendor was notified prior to evaluation or consulted during analysis. Findings represent the independent conclusions of the evaluation team. This site does not accept paid placements or sponsored reviews.

The Team

Meet the people behind the reviews.
