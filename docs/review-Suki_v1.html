Executive Summary

What You Need to Know

Suki is an ambient AI scribe built for enterprise clinical environments, designed to record patient encounters and generate structured documentation. The tool earned an overall grade of C+ (77.3/100), reflecting a product with genuine depth in integration and clinical coding — but carrying the highest concentration of Visibility of System Status violations across all four scribes evaluated in this review cycle.

Suki's standout strengths are in EHR integration and clinical coding support. It lists Epic, Oracle Health, athenahealth, and MEDITECH as supported platforms, and covers ICD-10, E/M coding, and CPT codes. Device coverage is comprehensive across laptop, desktop, phone, tablet, and web app, with explicit support for ambulatory, emergency department, and telehealth settings — a meaningful advantage for multi-setting deployments.

The most significant concerns center on Visibility of System Status (H1: 9 violations) and both Consistency and Standards and Match Between System and Real World (H4 and H2, each with 5 violations). Together, these findings indicate that users may frequently be uncertain what the tool is doing, encounter inconsistent language or interface behavior, and face a steeper-than-expected learning curve. With 11 major findings and 28 total findings — the highest total count in this evaluation cycle — Suki's usability profile warrants careful review, particularly for workflows where documentation errors carry patient safety implications.
