Our Process

Rigorous by design

Every evaluation follows a structured four-phase methodology developed specifically for clinical AI tools — combining established human factors engineering with real-world clinical workflow validation.

01

Phase 01

Clinical Workflow Development

We define and validate an 8-step outpatient workflow through direct clinician observation — from pre-charting to final sign-off.

02

Phase 02

Independent Heuristic Evaluation

Two blinded experts assess each product against Nielsen's 10 heuristics at every workflow step, then reconcile findings through structured consensus.

03

Phase 03

Structured Synthesis

Findings are organized by workflow stage, heuristic category, and severity — producing visual summaries that highlight patterns across the interface.

04

Phase 04

Clinical SME Validation

A physician with clinical informatics expertise conducts a task-based review to confirm findings are meaningful from a practicing clinician's perspective.

Why This Matters

Usability is a patient safety issue.

AI scribes are not standalone algorithms — they are interactive tools embedded within clinical workflows.

The rapid expansion of ambient AI scribes has outpaced independent, public usability assessment. Most evaluations focus on model accuracy or time saved — without examining how clinicians actually interact with these systems.

Usability failures at any stage of the documentation workflow can affect error detection, appropriate reliance, and documentation integrity — even when the underlying model performs well.

Who's behind the evaluations

Our team combines formal human factors engineering training with clinical informatics expertise to assess these systems against standards that matter in practice.
