Freed

A capable documentation tool with solid efficiency scores and reliable note generation, but meaningful gaps in system transparency and error prevention warrant careful consideration before deployment in complex clinical settings.

Evaluated using our independent human factors framework. Vendor-free.

B- 81.2 / 100
Overall Score
Executive Summary

What You Need to Know

Freed is an ambient AI scribe that records clinical encounters and automatically generates structured notes, designed to reduce documentation burden for outpatient providers. The tool earned an overall grade of B- (81.2/100), reflecting solid performance across most usability dimensions while carrying notable weaknesses in a small number of critical areas.

Its strongest quality is note generation flexibility and device coverage. Freed supports laptop, desktop, phone, tablet, and web app use, and produces notes with post-generation editing, SOAP formatting, and template support. EHR integration is listed as available, though specific details on supported platforms and integration depth were not publicly documented at the time of evaluation.

The most significant concerns center on Visibility of System Status and Error Prevention, two heuristics that directly affect how clearly a tool communicates its operational state and how proactively it guards against documentation errors entering the patient record. With 9 major findings and zero critical findings, Freed presents a moderate risk profile. It is well-suited for many general practice settings, but providers in higher-acuity environments should review their documentation verification workflows carefully.
